How performance is scored. Only verified Hits count. Each counselor is compared with peers of the same skill and type (SIM or Normal); team leaders with team leaders of the same skill. Each Hit count gets a percentile in the peer group, and the score (0–100) = 40% Applications + 40% Admissions + 20% Post-admission. A team's score is the average of its counselors; a coordinator's score is the average of all their counselors. Top 75+Above median 50–74Below median 25–49Needs attention under 25
