How is this scored?

Only approved claims (Hits, approved at both steps) count, with all claim types counted equally. Everyone is compared only with their own peer group:

  • Counselors: same skill and same type (SIM or Normal). Someone alone in their group is compared with everyone in the same skill.
  • Post-admission team: compared only with each other.
  • Team leaders: team score = average of their counselors' scores.
  • Coordinators: score = average of all their counselors' scores.

Score is a percentile of approved claims from 0 to 100: a score of 75 means more approved claims than about three quarters of the peer group.
